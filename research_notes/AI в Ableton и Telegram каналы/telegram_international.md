# Non-Russian Telegram channels on AI music and music production, plus key newsletters and Discords (as of Oct 2026)

> **How these were checked (read first):** In this research session the network proxy blocked direct fetches of `t.me`, `t.me/s/…` previews, tgstat.com, telemetr.io, Wikipedia and dynamoi.com (CONNECT 403 / ENOTFOUND). Every handle below is confirmed only because a search engine had indexed its t.me, telegram.me, tgstat or Nicegram page, so we know the channel existed when that page was indexed. **None of the channels below could be checked live for current activity or subscriber count.** Status labels:
> - **EXISTS (indexed)**: a t.me, telegram.me or TGStat page for this handle shows up in search results.
> - **UNVERIFIED**: mentioned only by a third party (a video description, for example), with no indexed channel page.
> - Activity: "unknown" unless a dated post was visible in a snippet.

## Which English Telegram channels post about AI music tools and news (Suno, Udio, stems, AI vocals)?

### Takeaway
No sizeable English-language Telegram channel that covers AI music *news* turned up. The Suno and Udio Telegram channels in the index are tiny fan or tutorial channels with a few hundred subscribers. The English music-production channels on Telegram are mostly about Ableton tutorials and packs, and many sit close to plugin-piracy channels. In the English-speaking scene, AI music news and discussion happens on Discord, Reddit and newsletters, not on Telegram.

## Ukrainian and other non-English channels on music + AI worth noting?

### Takeaway
No Ukrainian-language Telegram channel devoted to AI music could be confirmed. The one lead is a general AI-trends channel linked from a Ukrainian Suno tutorial video. The big Ukrainian music channels share human-made music, not AI content. There is one Spanish-language Ableton channel.

### Cited Findings
- **@aitrendchannel** (https://t.me/aitrendchannel) is linked from the description of a Ukrainian-language YouTube tutorial, "Генеруємо музику українською SUNO v5". **UNVERIFIED**: no indexed channel page was seen, and it is likely a general AI channel, not a music one — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLS87E24k0U)
- Large Ukrainian music channels (human-made music, not AI): "Українська Музика 🇺🇦" at ~161.5K subs and "Українська музика | Ukrainian music" at ~15.6K — [t.me/ukrainianmusiiicc](https://t.me/ukrainianmusiiicc), [telegram.me/TopUA](https://telegram.me/TopUA), [telegram.me/song_ua](https://telegram.me/song_ua). There is also "Ukrainian Company" @uacompany_music on Ukrainian TGStat — [uk.tgstat.com](https://uk.tgstat.com/en/channel/@uacompany_music). None of these has an AI focus.
- Ukrainian-language Suno guides exist on the web (not Telegram) — [GoIT: Suno AI українською](https://goit.global/ua/articles/suno-ai-shcho-tse-take-i-iak-stvoryty-pisniu-z-tekstu-za-khvylyny/), [ai360.com.ua](https://ai360.com.ua/vsoho-za-kilka-sekund-suno-ai-stvoriuie-muzychni-kompozytsii/)
- On Ukrainian TGStat, the AI-music content that surfaced was an ad in the *crypto* category for a paid course on building AI-music YouTube channels with ChatGPT + Suno, written in Russian. That is a monetization scheme, not a music channel — [uk.tgstat ratings](https://uk.tgstat.com/en/ratings/posts/crypto/py?sort=forwards)
- Spanish: "Ableton Live 11 en Español", @abletonlive11 — EXISTS (TGStat); subs and activity unknown — [TGStat](https://tgstat.com/channel/@abletonlive11)
- Other languages: a Hindi/Bollywood stems channel, "BAC Stems and Acapellas Updates" (@BACStemsAndPellas), shares stems and acapellas and is not AI-focused — [t.me/s preview](https://t.me/s/BACStemsAndPellas?before=17)

### Inferences
- The AI-music Telegram ecosystem is overwhelmingly Russian-speaking (another researcher covers that). Ukrainian creators seem to use YouTube plus general AI channels, not dedicated AI-music channels.

- Could not confirm whether @aitrendchannel exists or what it covers.

## Official channels of music AI products on Telegram, if any?

### Takeaway
None found. Suno, Udio, Mubert, Riffusion, ACE Studio and Musicfy all run their official communities on Discord (and Reddit, X and YouTube), not Telegram. Any Telegram channel or bot called "Suno" or "Udio" should be treated as unofficial and a possible impersonation.

### Cited Findings
- No official Telegram channel was found for Mubert, Riffusion, ACE Studio or Musicfy; their official presence is their websites — [mubert.com](https://mubert.com/), [SoundGuys roundup](https://www.soundguys.com/best-ai-music-generators-134781/)
- Suno's official community is on Discord: ~396K members per Discord's discovery page (created Jan 2023), ~395.9K per Discord Bot List, ~402.6K per Dynamoi — [Discord: Suno](https://discord.com/servers/suno-1069381916492562582), [discordbotlist](https://discordbotlist.com/servers/suno-1069381916492562582)
- Udio's Discord has ~16.8K members on Discord Bot List and ~17.6K per a third-party directory — [discordbotlist UDIO tag](https://discordbotlist.com/discord-udio-tag-servers)
- The Telegram "Suno" bots, such as Suno-AI-BOT, are third-party projects built on the Suno API — [GitHub](https://github.com/Malith-Rukshan/Suno-AI-BOT)
- Context for Oct 2026: Music Ally reports Suno is "far beyond" 2M paid subscribers and $300M in annual revenue — [Music Ally, 2026-10-02](https://musically.com/2026/10/02/suno-is-far-beyond-2m-subscribers-and-300m-of-annual-revenue/). Suno launched v5.5 with voice capture — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-launches-v5-5-ai-model-with-voice-capture-and-personalization-features/). Udio downloads (audio and stems) have been disabled since its UMG deal of 29 Oct 2025 — [search summary citing SoundGuys/rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-music-generators-2026)

### Inferences
- If the project wants "official" sources, follow product Discords and blogs, not Telegram.

### Gaps
- Could not check the social links on official sites (suno.com, udio.com, elevenlabs.io) because direct fetch was blocked. A Telegram link added recently would not show up here.
